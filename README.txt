JEWELLERY STORE - GENERIC DEMO WEBSITE
=====================================

This is a neutral demo template intended to show different jewellery businesses a possible website design. It contains no specific store name or business social-media account.

Files
- index.html: page content
- style.css: responsive design and layout
- script.js: mobile navigation and current year

How to preview
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3. Internet is needed for Google Fonts and example jewellery photos loaded from Unsplash.

Before publishing for a client
- Replace YOUR STORE and generic text with the business's approved name and details.
- Replace sample photos with the jeweller's own product photos or licensed images.
- Confirm product names, metal/material details, prices, stock, delivery, returns and contact details.
- Replace the sample quote with verified customer reviews or remove it.
- Add the business's real WhatsApp, phone, Instagram and address links.
- The demo is a visual showcase only; it does not include checkout, inventory, payment processing or order management.

Use this as a demo when approaching different jewellers. Do not present it as a live or official website for any store until the owner approves the custom version.
